# Output content

Owns what a final sheet carries.

## MUST
- Keep notes, chords, tempo, section marks, repeats, band cues and hint lyrics. Why: the sheet is for performing.
- Identify the song by filename. Why: the page carries no title.

## NEVER
- Never print a title, composer or performer credit, copyright line, contact, watermark, header, footer or page number. Cost: the sheet stops being music only.
